The promise

The Lite launches free for everyone and stays that way: no price, no fee, no subscription, no trial that expires. A paid Pro will exist for what a DJ needs once they grow (stems, recording, DVS) - never for the essentials of mixing. The commitment is versioned in the repository and overrides any other text.

The ask

The real bottleneck is the AI model capacity that the team of four agents (CTO, Coder, QA, Growth) consumes. Every engine decision, every reference study and every test iteration costs tokens. That is what gates the release.
